Ek design decision jo reference doc se alag hai

Reference doc kehta hai page signals ko selection override karni chahiye. Yahan explicit selection hamesha jeetti hai — kyunki dropdown ek promise hai: AU chunne wale ko Australian report milni chahiye. Page ke ulte signals silently override nahi karte, wo ek finding ban jaate hain. Selection na ho to reference doc ka order exactly lagta hai: ccTLD → schema country → page signals → html lang → US.

Pillar 4 · Security & Compliance
Cookie_Consent
us changed new rulePehle: banner nahi → fail, score 0, dono markets mein		 Banner ab bhi pass karta hai. Banner na ho to opt-out control dhoondta hai — "Do Not Sell or Share" link, sensitive-info limit, GPC. Mil gaya → pass. Nahi mila → warning (60), fail nahi. Same shape, par requirement alag: Australia mein cookie law hai hi nahi. APP 5 notice-at-collection aur APP 3 dhoondta hai. Banner na hone par AU site ab score down nahi hoti — yeh product ka sabse bada false positive tha.
Privacy_Policy (rights notice)
dictionaryPehle: checkGDPRCCPA — sirf gdpr / ccpa / "do not sell" dhoondta tha		 Regime = CCPA / CPRA + state laws. Keyword list mein original 10 terms jaise ke waise (GDPR samet — wo US site pe bhi lagta hai jo EU visitors serve karti hai), plus CPRA-era wording additive. Regime = Privacy Act 1988 + 13 APPs, regulator OAIC. Ab APP / Privacy Act / OAIC / notifiable data breach / access-and-correction terms dhoondta hai. Ek sahi Australian policy pehle fail hoti thi kyunki usmein "CCPA" shabd nahi tha.
Privacy_Compliance
new rule us changedPehle: sirf 2 signal (rights + data collection), policy ka content kabhi padha hi nahi jaata tha		 Base scoring waisa hi (100 / 70 / 0). Ab privacy policy ek isolated tab mein fetch hoti hai aur uska content coverage meta mein aata hai — par US mein score par asar nahi (overseasDisclosureRequired: false). Naya AU check: policy naam se batati hai ki data kin countries mein jaata hai? (APP 1.3(f)). Nahi → score 70 pe cap + named finding. Doc ke mutabik yeh AU dealer sites pe "almost universally missing" hai, aur US-hosted CRM/chat/analytics ke saath pair hota hai.
Legal_Disclaimers
new rulePehle: DISCLAIMER_GROUPS hardcoded Reg-Z / Reg-M / FTC — ek bhi AU term nahi		 Groups: finance (Reg-Z APR + triggering terms), lease (Reg-M), price (FTC + fee escapes), expiry. Required-by-page-type waisa hi: finance→[finance, price], lease→[lease, price], specials→[price, expiry], vdp→[price]. Groups: finance (comparison rate + prescribed warning), price (drive-away total, ACL s48), licence (ACL number, regex-matched), expiry. Lease group hai hi nahi — Reg-M ka AU analogue nahi. finance page → [finance, price, licence]. Component-only price ("+ on-road costs") alag se evidence mein record hota hai.
Pillar 8 · Conversion & Lead Flow
Pricing_Transparency
new rulePehle: fee-disclosure regex sirf US — doc fee | destination charge | out-the-door | tax, title		 Model = component-plus-fees. Middle 30 points fee disclosure se milte hain. Price labels: MSRP, sale/internet/selling price. Behaviour pehle jaisa. Model = single-total-price (ACL s48). Middle 30 points drive-away total dikhane se milte hain. Price labels: drive away, RRP. Aur component-only price bina total ke → score 45 pe cap + quotable finding, kyunki wahi cheez hai jispe ACCC action leta hai.
Vehicle_History
bug fix dictionaryPehle: sirf /carfax/ aur /autocheck/ regex — AU VDP 0/100		 Providers: CARFAX, AutoCheck (NMVTIS-backed). Language: accident-free, one-owner, clean title, clean carfax. Providers: PPSR certificate (PPS Act 2009), CarHistory, REVS. Language: ppsr checked, encumbrance, finance owing, not written off, stolen check, clear title. CARFAX Australia mein operate hi nahi karta.
TradeIn_Flow
bug fix dictionaryPehle: widget signals sirf kbb / blackbook / accutrade — complete AU tool ~zero		 Providers: KBB Instant Cash Offer, Black Book, J.D. Power, Edmunds. Vendor markers (accutrade, icoapi, instantcashoffer) US pack se aate hain. Providers: RedBook, Glass's Guide + domains. Locale mein ek payoutCheck bhi hai — encumbered trade-in ka PPSR payout, jo AU sites routinely omit karti hain aur jiska US analogue nahi.
Finance_Calculator
new rulePehle: calculator mila = flat 100, disclosure kabhi check hi nahi hota tha		 Text keywords waise hi. Naya: agar page ek rate/repayment quote karta hai aur APR nahi dikhta → 75 (warning). Reg-Z 1026.24. Keywords mein AU wording add — "repayment calculator", "weekly repayment", "car loan calculator". Naya: rate quote hai par comparison rate nahi → 75. Warning statement, "fees and charges apply", ACL number bhi meta mein record hote hain.
Financing_Flow
new rulePehle: finance flow mila = flat 100		 Koi licence disclosure requirement nahi (licenceDisclosure: null), to scoring bilkul pehle jaisa — flat 100. Naya AU check: finance offer karne wale page pe Australian Credit Licence number hai? Regex se actual 6-digit number match hota hai, sirf phrase se nahi — "we are a credit licensee" bina number ke exactly wahi case hai jo pakadna hai. Nahi → 75 + finding. NCCP Act 2009.
Certifications_Awards
dictionary us changedPehle: signals hardcoded — bbb, dealerrater, edmunds, kbb, ase certified		 Universal signals (certified, award, ratings, manufacturer-certified) + US credentials: ASE, NADA, BBB accredited, Google Guaranteed, CARFAX Advantage, DealerRater awards. Universal + AU credentials: LMCT / dealer licence, ABN, ACN, AADA, VACC, MTA-NSW/QLD/SA/WA, RACV / NRMA / RACQ, PPSR-checked, Certificate III in Light Vehicle. ASE, NADA, BBB — teeno Australia mein exist hi nahi karte.
Trust_Badges
dictionaryPehle: sirf generic security badges (ssl, secure, verified)		 Generic security seals + US credential marks alt/src text mein. Generic + AU accreditation marks. Ek AU dealer jo apna LMCT number aur VACC membership dikha raha hai wo strong trust signals display kar raha hai — jo purani list dekh hi nahi paati thi.
Reviews
dictionaryPehle: sirf class/id mein "review"/"rating" dhoondta tha, platform links kabhi nahi		 Ab outbound platform links bhi count hote hain: Yelp, Cars.com, DealerRater, Edmunds, KBB, BBB. Platform links: carsales.com.au, Autotrader.com.au, CarsGuide, Drive, ProductReview.com.au, Yellow Pages AU, True Local, ABN Lookup. Yelp aur BBB AU mein meaningless hain.
CTA_Clarity
dictionary us changedPehle: 11 verbs, "enquire" list mein tha hi nahi		 Global verbs (ab "contact", "call", "request", "schedule", "apply" bhi) + US idiom: get ePrice, value your trade, get pre-qualified, schedule service. Global + "enquire", "enquire now", "book a test drive", "get a drive-away price", "trade-in valuation", "book a service". "Enquire" AU ka dominant lead verb hai — pehle lagbhag har Australian dealer ka primary CTA "unclear" score karta tha.
Click_To_Call
bug fixPehle: fallback branch mein hardcoded 10–11 digit rule		 Valid lengths [10] (+ country code / trunk zero). Behaviour pehle jaisa. Valid lengths [10, 6]. "13 12 34" chhe digits ka hota hai — pehle reject ho jaata tha, to bina tel: link wala AU page 50 ki jagah 0 score karta tha. tel: link wala primary path dono mein waisa hi tha aur hai.

Honesty

US scores bhi thoda shift honge

Yeh bit-for-bit identical nahi raha. Teen jagah US behaviour badla, aur teeno correctness improvements hain — par aapko pata hona chahiye:

ParameterKya badlaKis par asar
CTA_Clarity Verb list mein "contact", "call", "request", "schedule", "apply", "shop", "find", "view" add hue. Purani list 11 verbs ki thi — adhoori thi, American nahi. Jis US page ka ekmatra CTA "Contact Us" tha wo 0 → 100.
Cookie_Consent Banner na hone par fail(0) ki jagah opt-out control dhoondta hai → pass ya warning(60). US mein bhi koi banner mandate nahi hai. Banner-less US sites 0 → 60 ya 100.
Privacy_Compliance Ek extra tab khulta hai privacy policy fetch karne ke liye (12s timeout, fail-open). Score par US mein asar nahi — sirf meta.policyCoverage add hota hai. Ek extra navigation per audit.
Certifications_Awards · Reviews "accredited" universal signals mein aa gaya; Reviews ab directory links par bhi pass karta hai. Kuch US pages thoda upar.

Agar aap chahte hain ki US bilkul purana behave kare, ye teeno gate ho sakte hain — bol dijiye.
