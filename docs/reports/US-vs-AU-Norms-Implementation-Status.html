Technical Performance

15 params · 13 identical · 2 interpretation-only · 0 threshold changes

Ek bhi number nahi badalta. Core Web Vitals Google ke constants hain aur log-normal curve Lighthouse ki hai. Jo differ karta hai wo physics aur data availability hai — AU audience jo US origin hit karti hai wo real latency penalty pay karti hai.

⚠️ Golden rule

AU ke liye kabhi adjusted thresholds mat banao. Agar LCP ya TTFB Australia ke liye relax kiya, to tumhare scores PageSpeed Insights aur Search Console se match karna band kar denge — wahi do tools jo har customer tumhein check karega. Market awareness diagnosis aur recommendation mein belong karti hai, kabhi bar mein nahi.

Security & Compliance

25 params · 18 identical · 7 changed · ~26% weight at risk

Do halves, ulta behaviour. Transport aur headers global engineering practice hain — 18 parameters, zero differences. Privacy, consent, finance-form aur disclaimer checks do bilkul alag legal systems pe baithe hain, aur galat rule set ke saath chalane pe dono false positives aur false passes dete hain.
